# PHASE 12 — Falsification (mandatory)

After a promising issue: **assume it is wrong. Try to kill it.**

Look for hidden authorization, impossible prerequisites, deployment differences, existing mitigations, economic offsets, thin liquidity, attacker losses, bad fork setup, wrong oracle state, token-behavior differences.

Attempt a counterexample to your own exploit inside the same harness. Record every attempt. A finding that survives this gets higher confidence. A finding with no falsification attempt cannot pass the report gate.
